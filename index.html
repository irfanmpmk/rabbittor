Rabbittor

Escape the cage, eat every carrot and reach the secret door before Big Mouth eats you.

How to play

  • The cage opensYou start locked in a cage at the maze entrance. After the countdown the cage opens and you can run.
  • Eat every carrot10 points each. Munch fast for streak bonuses.
  • Find the escape doorThe door in the middle of the maze stays locked until every carrot is gone. Run into it to escape.
  • Big MouthHe waits outside and comes in 10 or more seconds after you start. If he touches you, you lose a life.
  • InvisibleBig Mouth can't see you or catch you for a few seconds. Then it needs 2 minutes to recharge.
  • FighterEvery 30 carrots gives one charge. Use it, then touch Big Mouth to punch him all the way to the far side of the maze.
  • Golden carrotAppears sometimes for 10 seconds. Big points and 5 extra fighter carrots.
